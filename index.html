for you, adi.

sometimes i look at the moon and wonder if you’re looking at it too.

you’re in Myanmar and i’m here in the Philippines, and somehow we can still look at the same thing without even knowing if the other one is looking. i think there’s something beautiful about that. like no matter how far away you are, there are still little pieces of the same world that belong to both of us.

and maybe that’s what you’ve become to me. something that exists in the middle of my ordinary days, even when you’re not physically here.

i think about how 3 PM somehow became ours. it’s around the time we start calling, texting, playing, and before i know it, it’s 5 PM, then 12, then somehow 2 AM. time moves so strangely with you. when i’m with you, hours disappear like they were never there. but when we’re both busy and i can’t talk to you, somehow every minute knows how to take its time.

it’s funny because before you, i never really cared if my phone died. now i carry a power bank. i make sure i have data wherever i go. i check my battery like it’s something important.

i guess i didn’t notice when my routine started quietly making space for you.

i still remember thinking, why am i starting to care about Adan this much? we were literally just Roblox friends who sometimes talked on Discord. but then somehow i started thinking about you when you weren’t there. i started wanting to tell you things. wanting to play with you. wanting to hear what you had to say.

and then you became someone i looked for without even realizing i was looking.

i love listening to you talk about the things you like, especially when you get all nerdy about them. i could sit there listening to you yap about your interests for so long because i love seeing how excited you get when you talk about something you care about. and maybe it’s because i love games too, but sometimes even the most random things you tell me feel interesting simply because they came from you.

even your little Valorant moments make me smile. the way you get playfully pissed at the game and start complaining, but you’re not actually mad. i don’t know why, but i love those little pieces of you.

and i love how you baby me in games even when i’m doing absolutely nothing to deserve it.

especially when i lose and you tell me,

“it’s okay baby, i don’t need u to be good at the game. i just want you playing with me.”

i don’t think you realize how much that means to me.

because somehow, you always make me feel like i don’t have to be good at everything to be wanted there.

you just want me there.

and maybe that’s one of the things i love most about you.

i love your clingy side too, even when i tease you about it. i love when you call me cute, when you baby me, when you randomly say “i love you jelly,” or “ur a cutie pie jelly.” i swear, the way you say my name sometimes makes it feel like it has an entirely different meaning.

“Jellyyy”

there are so many ways to say my name, but somehow yours became my favorite.

and whenever i call you and you answer with “yes, my love?” i don’t know. something about it just gets me.

i love your laugh too.

especially when i say something stupid just because it crossed my mind and you laugh anyway. sometimes i think i say the dumbest things because i already know your laugh is waiting somewhere on the other side of it.

and your voice...

there are mornings when i wake up randomly and realize you’re already awake. and i’ll open my mic and mumble something like, “Adan, are u awake? good morning,” and hearing your voice right after waking up makes the whole morning feel different.

it’s like a little breath of fresh air before the day even starts.

sometimes we don’t even talk on call.

we’re just there.

and even in those moments, i still get this strange pull toward the thought of being beside you instead. not through a screen, not through headphones, not through a call. just sitting next to you and sharing the same silence.

i think about holding your hands, resting my head on your shoulder, looking at you while you talk, touching your hair, pinching your cheeks because i know i’d probably want to do that the second i finally saw you in person.

i want the ordinary things with you too.

a beach day where we don’t really have a plan.

roller skating even though one of us will probably embarrass ourselves.

taking random pictures with a digicam just so we have something to look back on years later.

walking around a park and talking about absolutely everything and nothing.

weekend movie nights where we argue about what to watch and somehow spend more time talking than watching.

just little things.

things that don’t sound extraordinary when you say them out loud, but i think they would mean everything to me if they were with you.

because that’s what you’ve made me realize.

i don’t just imagine big moments with you.

i imagine the small ones.

the kind that happen quietly and become memories before you even realize they’re worth remembering.

maybe that’s why you remind me of so many random things.

you’re my blue, because it’s your favorite color and somehow i guessed it right.

you’re my maraschino cherries and iced choco on a warm sunny day.

sometimes you’re my hot s’mores on a freezing night.

and you’re my 11:11 too.

that little moment when the clock lines up and you get to make a wish.

except sometimes i don’t think i’d need to wish for anything.

because somehow, i already found something i would’ve wished for.

you.

and i know that sounds like such a simple thing to say, but i think that’s what makes it true.

you made me want to write again.

you made me want to put my thoughts somewhere instead of letting them disappear in my head. you made me want to notice things again. little things. moments. words. feelings.

you listened when i talked about my past and the things that shaped me. you never made me feel like i had to hide those parts of myself to be cared for.

and i noticed that.

i noticed how gentle you are with me.

how you try to understand me.

how even when you get jealous, you still tried to respect my feelings because you knew what i had been through before. you didn’t have to tell me that part of you, but you eventually did, and honestly, i loved that you trusted me enough to let me see it.

not because i wanted you to be jealous.

but because it showed me that you were trying to care for me without forgetting how i might feel too.

and when you asked me to be your girlfriend, you told me you’d try your best to make me happy. you promised you wouldn’t lead me on or intentionally hurt my feelings.

i remember that.

i remember the reassurance in the things you said.

and perhaps that’s why being loved by you sometimes feels different.

because i don’t feel like i have to constantly wonder whether you mean what you say.

i can feel it in the little things.

in the way you listen.

in the way you stay.

in the way you make room for me.

in the way you somehow became part of my everyday life without either of us announcing that you were going to.

and yes, we still have so much to learn about each other.

there will be days where we misunderstand each other, days where distance feels heavier, days where things don’t go the way we want them to.

but i think i want to learn those things with you.

not because i think everything will always be perfect, but because i know you’re someone i genuinely want to keep knowing.

sometimes i get scared by how much i care.

because I used to think caring about someone this much means giving them a part of you that they could hurt.

but with you, i’m learning that being scared doesn’t always mean i should run.

sometimes it just means something matters to me.

and you matter to me.

probably more than i knew you would.

we always joke about who loves who more.

you always say it’s you and not me because it’s my own thought, so obviously you’re allowed to declare yourself the winner.

but secretly?

i still think i love you more.

i’ll let you believe otherwise, though.

just this once.

because if i tried to explain every little reason i love you, i think this letter would never end.

so instead, i’ll leave you with this:

if you ever look at the moon from where you are, i hope you remember that somewhere under that same sky, i might be looking at it too.

if you ever see 11:11, maybe you’ll think of me for a second.

if you hear someone laugh at something stupid, maybe you’ll remember how i always say random things just to hear yours.

and if you ever hear me say your name, i hope you know that somewhere along the way, Adan stopped being just a name to me.

it became a person i look for.

a voice i want to hear.

a presence i miss when it isn’t there.

my blue.

my 11:11.

my maraschino cherries and iced choco on a warm sunny day.

my hot s’mores when the night gets cold.

and, unfortunately for you,

my favorite stinky adan.

i love you, Adi.

more than i know how to fit into one letter.

and if i ever run out of words, i think i’ll just keep finding new ways to say it.


for you, i tried.

for you, i’ll keep trying.

always.
